Autumn careers fair and CV clinic

Quillmere Institute is a fictional institution. This material was written for the CourseFlow retrieval benchmark and describes no real course.

This notice is posted on every first-year module page by the Careers Service. It is not part of the DS101 teaching material.

The fair

The autumn careers fair takes place in the Great Hall on Wednesday 4 November 2026 from 11:00 to 16:00. More than sixty employers will be there, from small local firms to large public organisations, together with representatives of postgraduate programmes and volunteering schemes. Entry is free for students and graduates and no booking is needed for the fair itself.

First-year students sometimes feel that a careers fair is not for them yet. It is. Many employers run insight days and summer schemes aimed specifically at first-year students, and the fair is the easiest place to find out about them, ask what a typical day involves and collect contact details for later. Bring a notebook, prepare one or two questions about the kind of work you enjoy, and talk to at least one employer you had not heard of before.

CV clinic

Careers advisers will review CVs in the side rooms throughout the day. Each review lasts fifteen minutes. Bring two printed copies of your CV; places at the CV clinic are allocated in the order requests arrive, and late requests join a waiting list. Requests open on the Careers Service booking page on Monday 26 October at 09:00.

If you do not yet have a CV, come anyway. Advisers will help you plan one from scratch and point you to the templates and examples on the Careers Service pages. A first CV is usually a single page: your education, any work or volunteering, skills you can give evidence for, and two or three interests that say something about you.

Workshops on the day

  • 11:30 - Making the most of a careers fair in twenty minutes
  • 12:30 - Writing a cover letter that gets read
  • 13:30 - Applying for summer internships in your first year
  • 14:30 - Assessment centres: what happens and how to prepare
  • 15:15 - Building a professional online profile

Workshops are held in Seminar Room 2 and last forty minutes. Seats are limited and are allocated on arrival.

Accessibility

The Great Hall has step-free access from the east entrance, and a quiet room is available throughout the day. If you would like an adviser to meet you at the entrance, or you need any other adjustment, contact the Careers Service at least three working days before the fair.

Dress and what to bring

There is no dress code. Wear what you are comfortable talking to people in. Bring your student card, which is scanned at the entrance so that the Careers Service can send you the list of employers who attended and links to their vacancies afterwards.

Questions about the fair go to the Careers Service, not to the DS101 teaching team.
