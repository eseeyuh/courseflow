SND210 Sound Design for Media - Studio and module handbook 2026-27

Quillmere Institute is a fictional institution. This material was written for the CourseFlow retrieval benchmark and describes no real course.

Studio access

The department has two mixing studios and one recording booth. Students on SND210 may book up to six hours a week in the mixing studios through the studio booking system, in blocks of two hours. Bookings open each Monday at 09:00 for the following week. A booking that is not claimed within fifteen minutes of its start time is released to the waiting list.

Before your first booking you must complete the studio induction, which covers safe monitoring levels, the patch bay, the session backup procedure and what to do if equipment fails. The induction is offered in weeks 1 and 2 and takes ninety minutes.

Food and drink other than water in a sealed bottle are not allowed in the studios. Leave the studio as you found it: monitors off, chairs back, cables coiled, and your session copied to your own storage before you go. Files left on the studio computers are deleted every Friday evening.

Monitoring levels

Mix at a consistent, moderate monitoring level and take a break of at least ten minutes every hour. Ear fatigue changes what you hear long before it becomes uncomfortable, and decisions made at the end of a long session are often reversed the next morning. The studios have calibrated reference levels marked on the monitor controllers; use them.

Submission and late work

All coursework is submitted through the coursework portal. Work submitted after the deadline without an approved extension loses ten percentage points for each calendar day it is late, for up to three days; after that it receives a mark of zero. Large audio files can take a long time to upload, so start your upload at least an hour before the deadline.

To request an extension, use the Student Hub extension form before the deadline and attach any evidence you have. Equipment failures in the studios during the last week before a deadline are logged by the technicians and are taken into account automatically; you do not need to apply separately for those.

Academic integrity

Each mix must be your own work. You may use the music and effects supplied with a brief, and royalty-free material whose licence allows it, provided you list every source in your production log. Using another student's session or mix, in whole or in part, is academic misconduct.

Session files and backups

Keep every project in a single folder that contains the session file and all of its audio, with consolidated media rather than links to files elsewhere on the computer. A session that refers to audio in a downloads folder will open on your laptop and fail on the marker's computer. Before submitting, copy the folder to another machine or a fresh account and open it there; if any file is missing, the digital audio workstation will tell you.

Back up at the end of every studio session, to two places that do not depend on each other, such as your institutional cloud storage and a portable drive. Studio computers are wiped weekly, portable drives get lost, and cloud uploads of large sessions sometimes fail silently. A backup you have not checked is not yet a backup.

Name files so that their order and purpose are obvious: a date, the project, and a short description, for example 2026-10-20_podcast_rough-mix. Avoid names such as final, final2 and final-really, which tell nobody, including you a week later, which version is current.

Health and safety

Hearing damage is cumulative and permanent. Keep headphone levels moderate, especially when monitoring in a noisy environment, and never put headphones on while audio is playing at an unknown level. Report any equipment that produces unexpected loud noises, such as a crackling cable or a faulty power supply, to the technicians immediately and stop using it.

Cables on the floor are a trip hazard. Route them along walls or under mats, and do not leave stands where people walk. The fire exits from both studios must be kept clear at all times.

Equipment loans

Portable recorders, microphones and headphones can be borrowed from the equipment store for up to three days. Report any damage when you return an item; damage that is reported is never charged to students, but unreported damage may lead to a suspension of borrowing rights.

Contacts

Studio technicians can be found in the technical office next to Studio A during opening hours. Questions about briefs go to the module discussion board. Extensions and timetabling are handled by the Student Hub.
